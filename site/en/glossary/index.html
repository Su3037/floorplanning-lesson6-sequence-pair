Glossary

Terms and symbols

Every term is defined in the lesson before it is first used; this page collects the Chinese and English names and what not to confuse each with. Note: block D is one of the example’s modules and has nothing to do with the D in “DAG” or “DP”.

TermMeaningDo not confuse withFirst explained
floorplanning
布图规划		Place every rectangular module (and choose its shape) inside the chip area so that nothing overlaps, everything fits the outline, and the result is compact with short wires.Placement usually means thousands of standard cells; floorplanning handles a few large blocks.§ problem
module / block
模块（块）		A rectangle of fixed width w and height h, named A–E here. Its coordinate (x, y) is the lower-left corner.“Module 06” is the name of this course unit, not the geometric object.§ problem
fixed outline
固定边界		The allowed area W × H, here 10 × 8. Every block must lie inside [0, W] × [0, H].The bounding box is what the blocks actually occupy and changes with the pair; the outline is given and fixed.§ metrics
sequence pair
序列对		Two permutations (pos, neg) of the same block ids. The order of any two blocks in both decides whether they are left/right or below/above.A sequence pair encodes relative positions, not coordinates; coordinates come from longest paths.§ rule
pos and neg (Γ⁺ and Γ⁻)
pos 与 neg		The two permutations. The literature also writes Γ⁺, Γ⁻ or S1, S2.Some textbooks mirror the vertical rule; that equals this lesson’s convention with pos and neg swapped (see § limits).§ rule
relative position
相对位置关系		One of four for every pair: left, right, below, above. Here: before in both → left; before in pos, after in neg → below.“Left of” does not mean touching; it only requires x[j] ≥ x[i] + w[i].§ rule
horizontal constraint graph (H)
水平约束图		An edge i → j for every “i left of j”, meaning x[j] ≥ x[i] + w[i].No incoming H edge does not mean leftmost; it means nothing must be to its left, so x = 0.§ graphs
vertical constraint graph (V)
竖直约束图		An edge i → j for every “i below j”, meaning y[j] ≥ y[i] + h[i].Each pair goes into exactly one of H and V, never both.§ graphs
directed acyclic graph (DAG)
有向无环图		A directed graph with no cycle. H and V are DAGs because every edge points forward in pos.Acyclicity is guaranteed by the permutations; no separate check is needed.§ graphs
longest path
最长路径		The largest total weight of a path into a node of a weighted DAG. Here the weight is the predecessor’s width (or height), and the result is the smallest feasible coordinate.Taking the maximum is not about spreading blocks out: every incoming edge is a lower bound and all must hold.§ dp
topological order
拓扑序		An order of the nodes in which every edge points forward. pos itself is a topological order of both H and V.A topological order is not unique; following pos keeps the trace predictable.§ dp
packing
布局（压紧结果）		All coordinates computed from a pair. Each block slides as far left and down as its constraints allow.Different pairs can give the same packing: 3 pairs share the instructor’s packing.§ encoding
bounding box
包围盒		The smallest rectangle from the origin to (max(x+w), max(y+h)). The instructor’s pair gives 9 × 4.A small box does not mean it fits: 12 × 3 has area 36 too, but is wider than 10.§ metrics
overlap
重叠		Two blocks whose intersection has positive area. Sharing an edge or a corner (touching) is not overlap.A packing from a sequence pair never overlaps; fit is what must be checked separately.§ metrics
fits the outline
装得下		Bounding-box width ≤ W and height ≤ H. 13,680 of the 14,400 five-block pairs fit.Non-overlap and fit are two questions; a valid pair can still overflow.§ metrics
outline density / dead space
边界占用率 / 空白面积		Total block area ÷ outline area (23 / 80 = 28.75 %), and 80 − 23 = 57.Both numbers are the same for every pair and cannot compare two pairs; use bbox area or utilisation.§ metrics
bounding-box utilisation
包围盒利用率		Total block area ÷ bounding-box area. The instructor’s pair gives 23 / 36 = 63.89 % and it changes with the pair.High utilisation can still overflow the outline; read it together with fit.§ metrics
neighbour move
邻居（排列改动）		Swap two blocks in one permutation, or in both, to get a new pair. Simulated annealing walks randomly between such neighbours.One move may leave the packing unchanged, or move many blocks.§ encoding
